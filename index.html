How to Import Your WhatsApp Chat

Follow these 3 simple steps on your phone:

1

Export from WhatsApp

Open any conversation in WhatsApp → tap the 3 dots menu → More → Export Chat (Include Media for photos & videos).

2

Save the .ZIP or .TXT file

Save the exported archive file to your phone's File Manager or Downloads folder.

3

Open in ReChat

Launch ReChat, tap the Import Chat button, choose your file, and select your name to instantly align your messages!
